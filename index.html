02 / WHY CMPCT EXISTS

Most archives compress files.
CMPCT compiles relationships.

Traditional archives tend to treat paths as payloads to compress. CMPCT searches for exact relationships between required objects, chooses physical roots and records deterministic reconstruction paths while bounding locality and recovery cost.
