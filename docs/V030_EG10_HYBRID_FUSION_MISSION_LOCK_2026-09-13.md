## Mechanism hypothesis

A hybrid can preserve EG08 exactly while retaining most EG09 speedup:

1. first-pass fuse adaptive effort into requested-level-19 ordinary final packs exactly as EG09;
2. preserve every requested-level-3 probe and cold-stream emission at EG07 level 1 during first pass;
3. after the archive is structurally complete, parse authenticated `stream_packs` and identify cold stream indices using the exact EG08 stream-role law;
4. run the unchanged effort ladder **only on those cold stream packs**;
5. copy every ordinary pack and hot stream root payload byte-for-byte through that narrow rewrite.

This avoids a second decompression/recompression of ordinary packs while closing the one representation class EG09 cannot distinguish safely during first-pass compression.
