Status: **preserved breakthrough seed / not product or release authority**

This note freezes the first end-to-end evidence for the complementary Analytics ownership experiment and the first hostile locality result. It exists so future work cannot preserve the density headline while forgetting the exported locality debt, or discard the density mechanism merely because the first packaging boundary is not promotable.

The canonical product remains unchanged. No numeric project version, shipping selector, reader grammar or ONE evidence is modified by this seed.

## Mission lock

Genesis reactivated v0.30 because the frozen v0.30 line retained much of v0.29's density at a fraction of its creation cost, while ONE-G0.2 was not yet density-competitive. The largest post-pivot question is therefore not “compress everything harder.” It is whether v0.30 can recover mature predictive structure selectively, with marginal information yield high enough to preserve its compute advantage.

R4 had already established one exact Analytics relationship between `events.csv` and `events.jsonl`. A separate exact relationship also exists between the external `features.npy` and the `features.npy` member inside `features_compressed.npz`. The complementary-owner hypothesis is that each relationship should be represented once rather than storing both logical views independently.

Disproof conditions for the seed were:

- the reconstructed user tree differs by one byte;
- the dual owner fails to beat ordinary current-branch v0.30 on the identical Analytics tree;
- it fails to beat the accepted v0.29 Analytics artifact after all research-wrapper bytes are charged;
- the apparent gain depends on changing the workload, comparator or semantic tree.

## Regression-debt ledger

### Preserved breakthrough

- exact Analytics tree;
- 4,453,187 B research-wrapper size;
- -5,939,305 B versus ordinary current-branch v0.30;
- -1,681,985 B versus accepted v0.29 Analytics;
- no evidence that creation compute must return to v0.29-scale brute force.

### Open debt

1. **Full-matrix generalization.** The mechanism must fall back byte-for-byte on non-admitted workloads and retain a positive aggregate result on all 15 frozen workloads.
2. **Selective locality.** The exact NPZ owner as currently packaged is nowhere near the <=8x 4 KiB cold-read target.
3. **Physical I/O.** The earlier integrated owner only modeled locality; a rehabilitated design must instrument actual touched bytes.
4. **Integrity/recovery.** The research wrapper hashes components but is not the final canonical recovery/transaction boundary.
5. **Native/portable reader.** No new owner grammar has native/C-ABI/platform parity.
6. **Proof traffic/admission.** Generic opportunity detection and exact proof must remain bounded and corpus-independent.
7. **Repeated resources.** Creation/read/RSS conclusions need repeated fresh-process evidence and clear process-tree accounting.
8. **Reader complexity.** Any seek/checkpoint scheme must be bounded, independently testable and portable; a giant opaque restart index is not a free win.

## First rehabilitation hypothesis

A conventional full-window DEFLATE restart index is an obvious first idea: place checkpoints close enough that a 4 KiB request traverses <=32 KiB of compressed payload, and retain the necessary inflate state/history at each checkpoint.

That idea is now being tested as a **budget falsifier**, not assumed to work. The seed has only 1,681,985 B of Analytics margin versus v0.29. A textbook restart family that requires roughly one 32 KiB history window per <=32 KiB of compressed traversal may consume the density gain before authentication, bit state and framing are charged. If so, the correct next move is a representation-boundary change or smaller sufficient state, not weakening the locality target.
