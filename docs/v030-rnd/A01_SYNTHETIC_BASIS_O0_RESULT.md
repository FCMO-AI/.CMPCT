The preregistered positive threshold required >=1% and >=4 KiB complete-family improvement over the best observed single root. The latent root exceeded that threshold materially: **23,886 B / 12.33%**.

The hostile controls behaved directionally correctly. Independent random data strongly rejected synthesis. On the deliberately multimodal two-cluster family, two observed roots beat one synthetic root by 34,088 B / 11.18%, showing that the oracle is not simply rewarded for manufacturing a root everywhere.

The biased-medoid control is informative rather than ceremonial: even after placing a real member close to the latent prototype, the synthetic root retained a smaller but still exact 5,167 B / 3.01% advantage.

## What changed in the worldview

Within this frozen O0 regime, the assumption that useful physical roots must correspond to real source objects is false.

A non-observed, fully charged latent byte root can reduce the complete exact family description even when:

- the root itself is stored and terminal-compressed;
- every member residual is charged;
- reconstruction descriptors are charged;
- the same residual and terminal machinery is used for observed-root controls;
- exact byte reconstruction is mandatory.

This is not merely a better choice among observed ancestors. It is evidence for a new ownership relation: **a family may be more economically represented by an explanatory object that never existed in the logical filesystem.**

## Strongest negative / self-critique

This is deliberately synthetic O0 evidence. The generator was built around an unobserved prototype, so it establishes *possibility and headroom*, not prevalence. It does not establish that real arbitrary-data families contain enough latent-root opportunity to justify a permanent mechanism.

The coordinate-wise byte medoid is also not yet a causal theory of latent structure. It works here because the frozen positive family uses same-length sparse substitutions. The result says a latent physical basis can matter; it does **not** say this particular synthesis rule is general.

The two-real-root control is intentionally strong on multimodal ownership but expensive because each observed root is fully stored. Future O1 work must compare against current canonical multi-root/Mosaic-style semantics where applicable, not treat this research encoding as a substitute for the product's strongest existing family mechanisms.

No runtime claim follows: the oracle gifts discovery/search wall time and temporary analysis memory.

## Decision

**A01 advances to an O1 causal/transfer question.** Do not productize this exact oracle and do not claim release credit.

The next decisive experiment should ask whether a content-derived latent-root opportunity remains after replacing the friendly generator assumption with generator-distinct family structures and comparing against the strongest canonical observed-root/multi-root controls. It must include at least one variable-length or alignment-perturbed family, one multimodal family, one near-medoid family, and unrelated hostile families.

The O1 admission question is not “does the synthetic root compress smaller after exhaustive synthesis?” It is whether a bounded content-derived statistic or proposal can identify families where paying for a latent root is likely to beat observed-root ownership before expensive construction, with explicit AOM and false-positive accounting.

## Reopening / kill constraint

If generator-distinct transfer or canonical multi-root controls erase the latent-root advantage, narrow or retire A01 rather than tuning this synthetic family. Any new synthesis rule must be preregistered against unchanged hostile controls and fully charge all decoder-visible facts.
