## Builder interpretation

This is a **concept-compression result in encoder discovery**: on the tested transferred regimes, useful shift-invariant nomination does not require preserving the mature 4,096-position sliding-minimum data structure. A bounded epoch minimum retains the observed decisive opportunity with roughly half the incremental selector elapsed on the positive-marginal rows and substantially less modeled state.

This does not prove universal equivalence to sliding-window minima. Under the frozen transferred regime and charged MIY corpus, the simpler statistic survives every required case and clears every preregistered efficiency bound.

## Hostile review / remaining debt

The strongest surviving criticism is **integration debt**. Native timing measures the candidate signal scheduler, while sparse/rescue index sizes and exact proof traffic are charged in the ledger. A production-quality fused observer will also pay native index lookup/update, nomination bookkeeping and interaction with already-required fixed observation work. Those operations may change cache behavior and elapsed time even when retained bytes are charged correctly.

Therefore this receipt does **not** promote epoch-min as a product-speed path and does not yet retire the tail-return 8 KiB selector implementation as the reproducible mature control. It advances epoch-min to a fused end-to-end observation/index Builder whose entire incremental CPU, memory traffic and retained state are measured against the mature discovery path on identical inputs.

Opportunity bytes remain discovery headroom, not stored archive-byte savings. No v0.29/v0.30 superiority, reader-visible relation, canonical format/version or release authority is created. The 2026-09-11 same-input 15-workload Genesis gate remains mandatory and has not been run early.
