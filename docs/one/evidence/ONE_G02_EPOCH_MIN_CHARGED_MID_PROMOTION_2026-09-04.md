## Mission lock / Referee

The mature rightmost sliding-minimum selector had already established useful shift-invariant reuse opportunity but exported substantial encoder-discovery compute and state. Earlier maintenance rehabilitation reduced that bill without changing its exact selector semantics, culminating in the tail-return 8 KiB counter/offset dispatcher. A causally different scalar **epoch-min** hypothesis then asked whether the useful discovery relationship could be represented by a much smaller sufficient statistic rather than continuing to optimize the sliding-window machinery.

The candidate keeps the inherited `cmpct-gear-v1` content signal but does **not** preserve the mature selector's internal algorithm. During starvation epochs it retains only the rightmost lowest Gear state and emits that minimum at bounded epoch pulses. This is encoder discovery knowledge only. Survivors must still be byte-proven before becoming generic exact-reuse Laws; no reader opcode, archive grammar or Surprise semantics change.

The decisive charged experiment was frozen in `benchmarks/one/one_g02_epoch_min_charged_miy.py` before result-bearing execution. Its comparison charges:

- native candidate-minus-Gear and mature-minus-Gear selector elapsed on the same runner;
- native reserved signal state;
- retained sparse/rescue index entries at 16 B per entry on both sides;
- exact verification plus extension read traffic;
- byte-verified reuse opportunity only, never hypothetical stored-byte savings.

On every mature-positive marginal row the frozen gate required all of:

1. candidate total opportunity >= mature full-minimizer opportunity;
2. candidate modeled discovery state <= 0.60x mature;
3. candidate incremental selector elapsed <= 0.65x mature;
4. candidate marginal opportunity per incremental selector millisecond >= 1.50x mature;
5. candidate proof reads <= mature proof reads;
6. no candidate exact opportunity on a row where both fixed and mature report zero.

Any single row failure blocked advancement; aggregate gains could not hide it.

## Builder interpretation

This is a **concept-compression result in encoder discovery**: on the tested transferred regimes, useful shift-invariant nomination does not require preserving the mature 4,096-position sliding-minimum data structure. A bounded epoch minimum retains the observed decisive opportunity with roughly half the incremental selector elapsed on the positive-marginal rows and substantially less modeled state.

This result does not prove that the scalar epoch statistic is universally equivalent to sliding-window minima. The important advance is narrower and stronger: under the frozen transferred regime and charged MIY corpus, the simpler statistic survives every required case and clears every preregistered efficiency bound.

## Hostile review / remaining debt

The strongest surviving criticism is **integration debt**. The native timing measures the candidate signal scheduler, while sparse/rescue index sizes and exact proof traffic are charged in the ledger. A production-quality fused observer will also pay native index lookup/update, nomination bookkeeping and interaction with already-required fixed observation work. Those operations may change cache behavior and elapsed time even when their retained bytes are charged correctly.

Therefore this receipt does **not** promote epoch-min as a product-speed path and does not yet retire the tail-return 8 KiB selector implementation as the reproducible mature control. It advances epoch-min to the next Builder stage: a fused end-to-end observation/index implementation whose entire incremental CPU, memory traffic and retained state are measured against the mature discovery path on the same inputs.

Other non-claims:

- opportunity bytes are discovery headroom, not stored archive-byte savings;
- no v0.29/v0.30 superiority claim is created;
- no reader-visible ONE relation changes;
- no canonical format/version/release authority is created;
- the 2026-09-11 same-input 15-workload Genesis gate remains mandatory and has not been run early.

## Terminal decision

**Advance epoch-min to fully integrated fused-observer evaluation.**

Do not return to isolated sliding-deque/ring/suffix micro-tuning merely because those paths are mature. Reopen the mature machinery as primary only if integrated epoch-min loses required opportunity, exports unacceptable false-positive/proof traffic, or its fully charged fused compute/state no longer clears a material advantage.
