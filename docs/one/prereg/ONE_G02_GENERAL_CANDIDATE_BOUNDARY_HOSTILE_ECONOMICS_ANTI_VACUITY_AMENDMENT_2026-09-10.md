## Referee finding

The original preregistration names transfer rows specifically to test ADD8, XOR, Fill, exact reuse, and unrelated-data rejection. A harness that allowed those named relation rows to silently fall back to Surprise could satisfy `general_wire_bytes <= surprise_wire_bytes` by equality and therefore earn an economically meaningless ADVANCE without exercising Crystallization.

That would be a vacuous pass, not evidence about Crystallization economics.

## Additive gate

Every transfer row must now prove that the structure named by the preregistered row was actually exercised:

- `tiny_add8_2b` -> at least one ADD8 root;
- `tiny_xor_2b` -> at least one XOR root;
- `tiny_fill_1b` -> at least one Fill root;
- `beneficial_add8_4k` -> at least one ADD8 root;
- `beneficial_exact_reuse_4k` -> at least one exact-reuse root;
- `incompressible_pair_4k` -> zero exact-reuse/Fill/ADD8/XOR roots;
- `mixed_hostile_tree` -> at least one ADD8 root from its named tiny relation opportunity.

The candidate still receives only the source-tree path. The benchmark does not provide relation hints, constants, source/target pairings, or expected operations to the builder.

`ADVANCE_GENERAL_CANDIDATE_BOUNDARY_ECONOMICS_PREFLIGHT` therefore requires both:

1. the originally preregistered semantic/determinism/ontology/full-byte gates; and
2. this `expected_structure_exercised` anti-vacuity gate on every row.

A missing intended structure is a HOLD even if the Law archive is byte-identical to Surprise-only.
