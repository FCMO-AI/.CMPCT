# CMPCT Research Governance History

Status: **historical ledger; not normative strategy law**.

Purpose: preserve how CMPCT's autonomous research operating system evolved so future agents do not depend on lost chat history and do not accidentally resurrect superseded process rules.

Current normative authority is `docs/FUNDAMENTAL_RESEARCH_DOCTRINE.md` plus the Forge-scoped `docs/RND_DOMINATION_RUBRIC.md` and ordinary repository safety/release law.

---

## Stage 0 — benchmark/performance-driven development

Earlier autonomous development naturally treated benchmark losses and product gaps as the main source of work. This produced useful engineering but increasingly encouraged local optimization of individual workloads.

The project already had strong safeguards against benchmark theater and required mechanism-level evidence, but research allocation remained heavily coupled to current red cells.

---

## Stage 1 — Breakthrough Rehabilitation

`docs/BREAKTHROUGH_REHABILITATION.md` introduced the principle:

> **No regression is a promotion boundary, not an exploration boundary.**

A reproducible high-upside mechanism could survive temporary ordinary performance/product debt while the inherited release floor remained hard for promotion.

This prevented bold mechanisms from being tuned out of existence merely to keep an intermediate matrix uniformly green.

That policy remains normative and is **not superseded** by the later Foundry/Forge system.

## Stage 2 — R&D Domination Rubric as whole-R&D operating system

The first `docs/RND_DOMINATION_RUBRIC.md` formalized:

- D0–D5 causal diagnosis;
- R0–R4 radicality;
- S1–S6 saturation/escalation;
- strict-gap accounting;
- a 100-point RPS;
- exact-futility preference;
- Referee -> Builder -> Hostile Reviewer;
- family retirement;
- strict 15/15 ZIP/Zstd domination as the central research objective;
- a rolling-three-activation structural-red requirement.

This was a major improvement over unconstrained micro-optimization. It correctly distinguished implementation, execution, search, representation and productization failures.

However, it still began from **known reds** and therefore remained fundamentally reactive. It was excellent at deciding *how to fix a measured failure* but did not guarantee EntropyGraph/Mosaic-style questions about relationships that no benchmark had yet forced CMPCT to imagine.

## Stage 3 — Foundry/Forge doctrine v1

The first `docs/FUNDAMENTAL_RESEARCH_DOCTRINE.md` separated:

- **Foundry** — thesis-driven fundamental invention;
- **Forge** — convergence/productization;
- O0/O1/O2 evidence frontiers;
- R5 information-model invention;
- persistent Fundamental Theses;
- Assumption Ledger;
- structural transfer;
- asymmetric intelligent encoder / bounded decoder;
- concept compression;
- external prior art as vocabulary;
- Reversible Structure Compiler as the first primary thesis.

This restored the missing golden-age principle:

> Benchmarks are product truth, not the exclusive generator of scientific questions.

### Weaknesses discovered during final adversarial review

Doctrine v1 still had important failure modes:

1. **authority fragmentation** — strategy was duplicated across doctrine, rubric, active thesis and scheduler;
2. **forced-thesis risk** — requiring a live fundamental thesis could manufacture novelty theater;
3. **headroom-without-prevalence risk** — giant wins on tiny structural niches could be overrated;
4. **portfolio bloat** — fallback could hide permanent nomination/parser/recovery/platform tax;
5. **negative-evidence fossilization** — retired families could become dogma if applicability scope was lost;
6. **research-tool theater** — building more compiler/oracle infrastructure could masquerade as scientific progress;
7. **lifecycle blur** — Foundry state continued through rehabilitation/convergence rather than handing successful invention to Forge;
8. **frozen-experiment ambiguity** — preregistration immutability needed explicit constitutional protection;
9. **external anchoring** — importing current papers too early could narrow ideation into other projects' vocabularies.

## Stage 4 — Finalized Research Operating System

The current `docs/FUNDAMENTAL_RESEARCH_DOCTRINE.md` supersedes doctrine v1 and is the final constitutional design from this refinement cycle.

Key final changes:

### Two engines + Custody plane

- Foundry invents/falsifies information models.
- Forge converges/productizes evidenced mechanisms.
- Custody protects evidence, hard invariants, preregistration, release locks and zero-history truth without competing for innovation credit.

### Thesis Initiation Gate

No active thesis is better than a weak active thesis. A candidate must establish worldview/capability delta, non-triviality, prior-art boundary, plausible headroom, addressable-opportunity hypothesis, cheap oracle, kill rule, survival sketch and complexity/carrying-cost hypothesis.

### Foundry heartbeat instead of thesis quota

An empty Foundry is allowed, but bounded Foundry Reviews are triggered by major retirements/milestones/plateaus/new evidence or prolonged Forge-only activity. A review does not have to activate a thesis.

### Clean lifecycle ownership

Foundry owns:

`QUESTION -> CHARTERED -> ORACLE -> CAUSAL_SEED -> TRANSFER -> HANDOFF_READY`

Forge owns productization after handoff.

### Addressable Opportunity Mass (AOM)

Research must distinguish conditional saving from how much arbitrary data is plausibly addressable by the structural predicate.

### Global mechanism carrying cost

Every mechanism is treated as a permanent tax until it proves otherwise: nomination/audition cost, metadata, parser/fuzz states, binary/startup, recovery/locality, native/platform burden and interaction complexity all count.

### Concept-compression ratchet

Prefer general primitives that subsume older special cases. Research DSL richness does not automatically become canonical reader richness.

### Frozen-experiment immutability

Once result-bearing execution begins, material scientific changes require a new superseding freeze. Unfavorable evidence may not edit the old experiment.

### Scoped negative evidence

A retirement is a conditional theorem with explicit tested regime and reopening predicates, not universal dogma.

### Breakthrough Evidence Vector

Foundry thesis quality is not collapsed into one score. Track capability delta, headroom, AOM, causal confidence, transfer, survivability, complexity/carrying cost and information gain separately.

### Research infrastructure no-credit rule

Tooling counts as scientific progress only when it enables or produces a decision-changing experiment.

### Independent ideation then external adversary

When practical, first generate a local assumption-driven hypothesis, then use external primary work to establish prior art, stronger controls and missing vocabulary.

## Scheduler governance transition

Earlier ChatGPT scheduled-task prompts duplicated large amounts of research strategy and current project facts. That created a second control plane that could become stale relative to the repository.

The finalized scheduler is intentionally a **thin execution kernel**:

- enforce productive hourly execution and handoff;
- recover exact repository truth;
- read the finalized doctrine, active thesis, frozen experiment and Forge law;
- continue the repository-owned thesis/convergence state;
- never become strategy authority itself.

Current facts, mechanisms, benchmark counts and research priorities belong in repository state/evidence, not hard-coded scheduler prose.

## Historical interpretation rule

When reading old commits, PRs, reports or task transcripts:

- “Domination Rubric governs all R&D” is historical Stage 2 language;
- “strict 15/15 is the research North Star” is historical Stage 2 language;
- “one structural-red advance every rolling three activations” is historical Stage 2 allocation law;
- “always maintain one live fundamental thesis” is superseded Stage 3 language;
- one long state chain extending a Foundry thesis through convergence/productization is superseded Stage 3 language.

These rules explain past decisions but do not override the finalized doctrine.

What remains invariant across all stages is the refusal to weaken evidence, hide competitor losses, use benchmark identity as production policy, trade away correctness/integrity, or call unmeasured debt solved.
