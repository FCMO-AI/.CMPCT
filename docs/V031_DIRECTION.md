# CMPCT v0.31 development direction

Status: **ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT LINE**

v0.31 starts from the published v0.30 checkpoint under `docs/PRE1_RELEASE_POLICY.md`.

There is no version-specific 15/15 completion quota. Work should pursue the highest-leverage measured
improvement available across compression, creation/extraction cost, execution ownership, representation,
portability and robustness. Publish v0.31 when it forms a coherent material checkpoint with honest
evidence and preserved losses.

The strict 15/15 size+create objective versus ZIP/Deflate-9 and solid Zstd-19 belongs to **CMPCT 1.0**.

Immediate inherited opportunities include reducing speculative r25 candidate/search cost, improving base
hot paths that still lose to ZIP, and converting promising representation headroom into complete
same-head product economics. Historical GENESIS/ONE and other prior approaches must be searched before
reopening a causal family so v0.31 does not rediscover old work under a new name.
