## Interpretation, disproof boundaries and alternatives
The preregistered exact-membership predicate survived this fixed-stride hostile-tail, different-stride, accidental-magic and valid-generation court. The new certificate stores at most 32 `(lo,hi,stride)` triples per operation and only skips offsets in its already-observed exact arithmetic progression. Successful chains never become negative certificates. It **does not** bound arbitrary interleaved/sparse malicious ancestry, mutation while Reader accesses the same inode (see [#654](https://github.com/FCMO-AI/.CMPCT/pull/654)), temporary I/O, cold startup, Windows/macOS behavior or global create/extract performance. Do not treat a passed test as license to relax formal parser limits, recovery contract, or the signed transaction validator.

A useful **information-model bound** explains why one constant-size proof cannot cover every possible arbitrary invalid-position subset. For `U` possible physical positions and `N` arbitrary known-invalid positions, an exact membership encoding needs at least `log2(C(U,N))` bits (before other proof semantics); for U around 1.329M and N=16,400 this is about **15.6 KiB** optimally, unless a structural predicate such as constant stride greatly narrows the family. The arithmetic-run seed is one structured compression option, *not* the general case. Follow-on architecture choices include a succinct exact sorted-position index with charged memory, an external exact spill/certificate with charged I/O, or an explicit work budget and **declared** fail-closed recovery limitation rather than a silent change of fallback semantics. #365 depth abort cannot simply be spliced into #650: [#658](https://github.com/FCMO-AI/.CMPCT/pull/658) already falsified that shortcut.
