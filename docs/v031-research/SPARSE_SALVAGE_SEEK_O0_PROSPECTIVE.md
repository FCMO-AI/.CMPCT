## Source of the hypothesis — already observed, not retrospectively preregistered
A local standalone **exploratory** instrument run before this document found full framing parity on sparse, dense and a cross-hole candidate. On a 4 GiB+64 KiB sparse logical file, a direct SEEK_DATA/SEEK_HOLE prototype read ~20 KiB of physical extents and returned the same four header candidates as a copied PR293 bounded scanner in 0.419 ms versus 11,886.86 ms median wall (two local samples/arm). This is NOT product evidence; no independent hosted claim or portable guarantee follows. A 16 MiB genuinely dense source made sparse-seek slower (10.33 versus 8.66 ms medians), establishing negative portfolio debt. Local scripts/results are diagnostic, not a substitute for the hosted prospective court.

## New prospective question and prediction
Can a **read-only** extent-aware physical-header enumerator use `SEEK_DATA/SEEK_HOLE` to avoid visiting logical zero holes, yet preserve all six declared historical header fields, physical candidate offsets, ordering, and *unverified* confidence labels for every candidate, including a full header straddling an actual sparse hole? The new same-source **hosted**, held-out court must compare against real PR293 `recover_blob_records` on independent sparse layouts, a >4 GiB logical source and dense/unsupported paths. It must not build a false `verified` content or committed-generation claim.

- Prediction: on Linux ext4-like hosted filesystem supporting coherent SEEK_DATA/SEEK_HOLE, the alternative produces **exactly** the existing scanner's candidate tuples and trust labels; reported physical bytes read are much smaller than logical bytes on sparse inputs, and a repeated same-input, same-process-start-semantics A/B reduces scanner wall and CPU work by orders of magnitude for the >4 GiB regime.
- Hard negative control: on actually dense physical input, SEEK_DATA may add overhead; either proactively decline fast-path admission by a source-only sparse statistic (for example `st_blocks`) or record honest regression. Never tune on workload names.
- Hostile boundary: do not scan only headers contained within one physical extent. Magic `CMA4` is nonzero, but the remaining declared header may logically span into zeros. Read the complete 64-byte logical header with `pread` when magic is found.
- No semantics change: returned records remain unverified candidate framing only, with no CRC/SHA check, decode, committed ancestry, extraction, output authentication or snapshot guarantee.
- Resource boundary: extent count and syscalls must be charged. Unsupported, inconsistent/nonforward extent reports, dense admission, or OS without SEEK_DATA must conservatively use the exact inherited scanner; seek skipping is a research oracle, NOT a new product mode.
- Kill: any missed/duplicate/reordered candidate, altered declared value, confidence escalation, failure to handle a hole-straddling header, mislabeled sparse/dense observation, or unaccounted I/O fails that claim. Non-portable filesystem SEEK_* errors narrow the claim to Linux/coherent supporting filesystems. Lack of held-out advantage means do not productize.
- Cost to carry: additional open/fstat/seeks/preads and filesystem dependency can erase wins for dense or highly fragmented inputs; no archive-byte win, random-read proof, recovery/authentication claim, platform parity, or release credit.

## Alternatives / Pareto BONK
(1) Current one-MiB readinto scanner: portable, bounded memory, but O(logical bytes) visits; strongest actual-product incumbent. (2) SEEK_DATA/SEEK_HOLE with conservative fallback: new physical-organization frame, potentially huge sparse win but filesystem-dependent. (3) mmap: address-space/page fault carrying cost; not a free skipping oracle. (4) Generic authenticated salvage: orthogonal, much higher work/risk. This episode tests (2) against (1) only. Discovery `STATE.json` owned by PR #240 remains untouched.

## Decision boundary / counterfactual
A sparse multi-GB fixture with two isolated records is a cheaper discriminator for seek-space leverage than any full archive benchmark. A single header deliberately spanning a page into a hole is the sharp correctness falsifier against naive extent-only scanning. Hosted scientific positive may justify separate owner-side production admission and cross-filesystem tests, not publication or release.
