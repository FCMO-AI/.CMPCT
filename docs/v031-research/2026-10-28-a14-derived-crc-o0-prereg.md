## Primary question and prediction
On arbitrary opaque fixed-stride binary records that actually contain a CRC32 of the other record bytes, can a content-only, bounded candidate detector find and *prove exact* a deterministic CRC field, then encode records without storing that field, obtaining strictly fewer **complete representation bytes** than both conventional solid Zstd-19 and a strong structure-only reversible transform, even when charging full decoder-visible parameters and a strong original SHA-256? 

Prospective positive criterion: at least **two distinct** generated CRC-positive regimes (different record width AND placement), with complete folded artifact (header+compressed bodies) **>=5% smaller** than the best of direct Zstd-19, direct LZMA-9/XZ equivalent, and stride-transpose-Zstd-19 after the latter's comparable header/parameters. At least one positive must use high-entropy payload. A positive is scoped O0 headroom only. If stronger conventional control wins, mark a negative, not a partial product victory.

## Deterministic blinded controls (definitions before outcome)
- Valid regimes: SHA256-derived 32/64/128-byte body records; 8,192 / 4,096 / 2,048 records; CRC32 stored at head / tail / fixed internal offset (4); fixed seeds 1401, 1402, 1403. A fourth structured 64-byte-body regime with 4096 records, seed 1404, tests nonuniform entropy.
- Hostiles: same generated byte shape but no CRC (seed 1405); one last-record checksum bit flipped after a legitimate prefix (seed 1406); variable-position checksum offsets per record (seed 1407). No false admission, and no roundtrip difference is allowed.
- Candidate detector receives ONLY bytes and a public fixed search budget: widths divisible by four in [16,256], field placement in {0,4,width-4} where valid, little/big endianness, <=32 sampled records per candidate, followed by **full exact validation of every record** before admission. No filename, fixture identity or known seed may affect admission. All discovery search CPU/wall is measured, not donated to product; the O0 gift is the bounded candidate grammar, not decoder-visible facts.
- Artifact format for this experiment only: explicit magic/version, width, field offset, endian, count, original SHA-256, compressed-body length and compressed-body bytes. No fixture identity or algorithm guess at decode. Require decoder to reject malformed headers, compressed-length mismatch and corrupt payload/invalid digest.
- Compare same input bytes with `zstd -19`, `lzma.FORMAT_XZ preset=9`, and record-wise transpose then `zstd -19`; charge representation metadata in the transpose arm. If CLI Zstd-19 unavailable, mark that competitor UNMEASURED and no positive decision. Measure raw input bytes, full stored artifact bytes, create wall including candidate scan/encode, decode wall, reconstructed SHA and optional resource information. Record exact zstd version.
- Prospective cost qualifier: positive headroom is not product viability until bounded generic admission, selective range decode, RSS, portability, false-positive prevalence, real held-out naturally occurring data and genuine CMPCT Builder/Reader are tested. Decoder regenerates field from payload and therefore cannot claim CRC32 as a cryptographic authenticity proof; SHA-256 is separately charged.

## Alternative explanation, kill/narrow conditions and contrafactual
Alternative: simply deinterleaving columns creates the apparent gain without field removal; the transpose control falsifies that. Another: solid XZ alone detects the structure; exact XZ-9 control adjudicates. Another: checksum-positive fixtures are unnatural; report that limitation as decisive external-validity debt, never infer general applicability from synthetic. A single wrong original byte, false admission on adversaries, missing comparator or unexplained sample discrepancy kills success regardless of apparent size.

Prospective process lesson to test later: an exact derived-field oracle should be run *before* product-specific codec implementation when a deterministic function of bytes is suspected; do not modify discovery policy from this one case.
