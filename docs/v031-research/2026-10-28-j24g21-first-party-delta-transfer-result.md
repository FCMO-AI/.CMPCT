## Scientific decision and counterfactual

**Narrow decision: do not promote default certified projection as an ordinary first-party Writer delta optimization.** First-party Writer produces no large ignored subtree, so the demonstrated owner savings of #691/#694 do not transfer automatically. Preserve the certifier/projection as a **specialized seed** for exceptionally large authenticated unknown fields; product promotion would need prospective generic admission and real, representative, fully charged whole-Reader economics with a strong direct-decoder control.

Counterfactual replay: reading the first-party \`transactions.py\` delta construction and extracting just one Writer-produced authenticated footer **before** the expensive 800k signed-extension RSS court would have shown the lack of producer-reachable ignored \`extension\` immediately. This is prospectively recognizable and transferable as *check producer reachability before extrapolating synthetic micro wins*. Not sufficient evidence to alter repository discovery policy from one episode; no Discovery Engine policy mutation.
