## Source-exact question / cause
J24F09 neutralized same-inode SIGBUS only for all-RAW S_CHUNKS/S_CDC staged extraction. At that exact head, `CMPCT.read_range()` on a directly stored RAW S_BLOB still executes `BHDR.unpack_from(self.mm, pos)` and slices `self.mm[base+start:base+end]`. After the Reader has mapped an authentic archive, a separate writer can truncate the *same inode* and a subsequent late-range request can fatally SIGBUS in Linux/macOS. Can the existing checked, descriptor-bound `_raw_blob_view_for_extract` source be reused for this public RAW range route without changing format/semantics or materializing the full blob?

## Alternatives and control
(A) Disallow mutable sources by contract: simpler but does not make a portable archive browser resilient to source truncation. (B) `fstat` before mmap: rejected as racy between check and dereference. (C) Full-file snapshot: O(archive) I/O/temp exported. (D) One descriptor-owned read of requested range, including a descriptor-read/checked physical header using existing helper; selected cheapest bounded change. **Strongest control:** exact historical J24F09 Reader (not reimplemented) on the **same builder-independent authentic r24 single RAW BLOB**; use crash-isolated process to contain baseline SIGBUS. A replacement *pathname* with different inode after open must not change returned bytes.
