## Primary question and causal contrast
Can **two or more distinct physical ZIP containers containing an identical decompressed payload but different exact valid Deflate streams** make O63's genuine VZIP representation materially smaller than the same-input forced-opaque fallback, even when the ZIP members exhibit a high physical-to-logical inflation ratio that made VZIP a CPU/byte loser in O68's singleton family? This is a test of cross-object reconstruction/ownership leverage, not a recommendation to accept any ratio policy.

- Positive prospective evidence: at least one high-ratio (>=32:1) related-container cohort for which true VZIP is chosen for **all** members, reconstructs every original ZIP exactly, and emits fewer complete archive bytes than the opaque arm by >=128 B. Preserve create CPU/RSS as incurred debt, not free savings.
- Negative falsifier: no related-container cohort beats opaque on complete archive bytes, or any successful byte improvement requires a non-equivalent source tree, invalid ZIP, non-reproducible Deflate, different Builder/codec, or 8+ ZIP cohort S_PACK routing. In this case, retire this *narrow* structural example, not all conceivable VZIP positive families.
- Stronger simple controls: one high-ratio ZIP (O68-like singleton); two **independent** raw payloads with comparable local compressibility, intentionally no shared decompressed identity; two equal raw payloads with exact same Deflate mode but distinct metadata; two equal raw payloads with two genuine Deflate levels. These controls distinguish generic ZIP header repetition and ordinary opaque compression from unique raw-content dedup.
- Alternative explanation: Zstd compression of concatenated physical ZIP bytes may already recover all available redundancy; VZIP may retain both Deflate variants plus recipe overhead, and opaque wins regardless of dedup.
- Distinct family/representation axis: cross-object content identity and physical-stream diversity rather than expansion ratio alone or eight-member cohort grouping.

## Cheapest legitimate evidence rung
Deterministically generate two to four real stdlib Deflate ZIP inputs per cohort, with fixed metadata and source SHA; keep count <8 to avoid inherited S_PACK policy. Include repeated text, mixed entropy, and dissimilar-but-same-compressibility independent payloads; vary level 1/6/9 and member names. Use exact same source tree, executable source/codec, and full archive semantics for both arms. On separately installed authentic O63, run repeated paired child-process Builder builds: VZIP arm unmodified, opaque arm with only a **research-interpreter replacement** of imported `make_vzip_recipe` returning `None`. Read all ZIPs back, verify SHA and whole archive, and independently parse their members. Never advertise the patched control as a product implementation.

For each raw observation capture source SHA set, physical ZIP bytes, member expanded bytes and compressor level, storage kinds, archive bytes and SHA, create wall/CPU, process RSS, and (if feasible) cold reader wall/CPU for source reads. Alternate A/B ordering with >=3 repetitions per arm on the same host. Compare complete stored bytes and the actual ratios, not compression-library intermediate sizes. For paired controls require the same source hashes in both arms and deterministic archive bytes within an arm. CPU/process startup boundaries are reported separately. Cap each raw payload to 2 MiB, two to four files per cohort, no mutable file inputs and independent process timeout. No expensive full release matrix is justified until a productizable owner survives this cheap counterexample test.

## Promotion / negative knowledge
Even a positive outcome is **research evidence**; it would falsify a universal physical inflation-ratio admission rule and motivate a prospective bounded cross-object signal with full product costs, but it cannot authorize a threshold, merge or release. An all-negative result bounds this constructed family only and favors an early CPU-budget guard plus stronger held-out positive controls. Raw observations, unexpected fallbacks, contradictory data and setup failures remain public. Neither a permissive evaluator nor a changed ZIP corpus retroactively rescues a prediction.
