## Mechanism and stronger simpler control
Change only one transaction generation's `_apply_delta` replay to use an operation-scoped mapping from canonical **literal** keys to min-heaps of surviving physical row positions. Preserve array order and prior first-match behavior. A rename moves the lowest live positional member; `put` replaces first surviving row of the exact same name or appends; `del` removes all matching rows; duplicates remain accepted during replay and rejected only by the unchanged later strict structural validator. No normalization, sorting, dedup or output canonization. Materialize the same final ordered files list and metadata before any further generation. Retain conservative original scan for malformed initial input when indexing cannot safely model source; later validator still rejects. Avoid new format fields, API, limits or reader edits.

**Cheaper alternative**: global dictionary path->row loses first-occurrence semantics under transient collision and cannot preserve ordering. Preemptive rejection on transient duplicate name would change accepted delta behavior; not allowed. A small-op fallback may avoid imposing heap costs on ordinary one-op deltas, but must be selected from defensible algorithmic bound rather than workload identity.
