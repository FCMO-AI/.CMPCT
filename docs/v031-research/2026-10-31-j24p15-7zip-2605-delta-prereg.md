## Primary question / hypothesis

An absent/unverifiable binary or wrong tree is an infrastructure/semantic FAIL, not a compression result.
H2 (observed delta, NOT predetermined direction): for each fixed structurally different family, record every complete archive size and SHA and raw process create/extract wall for both versions. Differences may be positive, negative, or zero; do not require a win.
H3 (control strength): 4 independently executed create/extract runs per arm, rotated ABBA order, source/path/mtime identical; original provider SHA, executable SHA, input tree SHA, host/process version and exact source HEAD in durable JSON. No median-only timing receipt.
Decision discriminator: if any material compressed-size delta or repeated process-runtime delta exists, prioritize new source-exact whole 15-row comparison before calling the current competitor unchanged; if none, retain 26.05 as current-world provenance debt for later full matrix, without claiming identical output on arbitrary datasets.
