## Question and falsifiable predictions

Is the simpler sorted unique DELETE bisect/pop method worth its extra product fast-path when compared with the more general name-map/sort-once factored replay method on **exactly the same** 63 one-operation delete histories, including pathological deletion positions and index sizes?

Baseline: original shared `_apply_journal_ops`, an O(N)-scan/filter/rekey/sort owner per generation. Separate existing evidence: J24G67 14.78–16.23x Reader-monkeypatch win on 2,048/63 deletion; J24G64 7.64–9.31x replay-only for a different mixed sparse-fsmeta input. Those ratios **cannot be compared**. The strongest control is the original helper applied to identical inputs to establish exact MessagePack semantics, exception parity and no input mutation. The general factored mechanism already handles mixed signed sparse-metadata and deserves simplicity/coverage credit.

**Prediction:** for N=2,048, 8,192, and 32,768, D=63, with first-/middle-/last-live-name deletion patterns, the simple method will be at least 20% faster in total one-chain wall time than the factored method on at least two of three hosts in the N=2,048 middle case, measured as median(factored)/median(simple) >=1.20. It should retain its edge for most larger cases. Factored may win at front-delete on large N due to `list.pop(0)` shifting O(N) references; preserving such a contradiction is required. This is a directional test, not a promotion threshold.

**Decision:** If the general factored method is within 15% of simple at 2,048/middle on at least two hosts and traced peak allocation does not exceed simple by >1 MiB, **prefer one general mechanism** instead of adding a specialized product branch. If simple beats factored by >=20% on at least two hosts **and** has <=1 MiB extra traced peak, keep it as a potential separately admitted product path pending full Reader/metadata/RSS evidence. Otherwise classify ambiguity/conditional, not victory. A >20% loss in a structural perturbation blocks a universal speed claim. Unknown opcode, malformed inputs and sparse metadata must still use inherited reference behavior, not be reinterpreted by this test.

## Instrument and exported-cost accounting

At each N/pattern construct one deterministic sorted unique index of opaque rows plus 63 single `del` generations and no sparse metadata. All variants consume the same immutable in-memory bytes, generated *once* per case; record source MessagePack SHA, final MessagePack SHA and baseline parity. Execute paired ABBA alternation (8 blocks at 2,048, 6 at 8,192, 4 at 32,768), preserving raw per-sample perf_counter_ns and process_time_ns. Warm-up excluded, gc collection before measurements. Deep-copy base and deltas in both variants, including sorting/materialization and admission. Do not compare a code-only loop with a full Reader timer; no process startup inside timer. On every case record both tracemalloc peaks on independent repeated runs and peak delta; tracemalloc is not OS RSS. Record the time to initialize and validate eligibility; do not gift either candidate a prebuilt index or map. No physical archive bytes are generated by this microcourt, so no storage advantage is claimed. Decoder-visible facts must match.

Controls: same-messagepack-result SHA and same input SHA after both algorithms; source-exact pinned competitor blob; no writes to product source; separate direct original replay parity for each case; at least one false-positive/unknown-op and one signed-xattr sparse metadata candidate exclusion inherited from J24G67 and J24G64.
