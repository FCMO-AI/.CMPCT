## Material causal uncertainty

Its selected-member wall observations necessarily mix compression, child process startup and decoder work; they cannot support a fair in-process Reader latency assertion. A second **same-archive, same-input, same-decoder** component court is needed to isolate whether the physical 16-frame ownership layout intrinsically exports per-member decode cost even after process startup is removed.
