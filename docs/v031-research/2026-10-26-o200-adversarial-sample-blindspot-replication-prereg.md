This is a causal blind-spot question, not a new feature-policy proposal. It challenges the sufficiency of bounded fixed-window compressibility rather than tuning O199's threshold.

**Predicted discriminator:** the selector makes the *same decision, same shift offsets and same sampled zlib ratio* for original and camouflaged pairs, yet in >=4/6 camouflaged held-out cases the native charged proxy becomes strictly larger than strongest fixed while the original random family still wins.
