## Root mechanism and counterfactual

With 1MiB payloads plus a 128-byte charge, 16MiB cold FIFO holds **15** entries. After an ascending 96-key first pass, the FIFO holds the final 15 and the 64-name ghost holds at most the 64 preceding evictees. The earliest 17 keys are already unrecognized. The second ascending pass begins with precisely those missing keys. Their misses displace the 15 cold residents and consume ghost FIFO positions **before** the previously remembered keys are revisited. A cascade leaves **zero** second-forward-pass reuse: the algorithm cannot distinguish a repeated scan from never-before-seen keys with this short history horizon.

The cheap counterfactual discriminator could have been applied **before implementation**: estimate the minimum reuse-distance history needed to recognize any return after scanning N distinct keys, given C resident cold keys. In this trace `N-C=96-15=81` identities must fit the recent-history horizon, while this preregistered policy remembered only 64. This is a useful prospective research-design lesson, **not** evidence that raising the ghost parameter to 81 would produce a generic win. Retuning after seeing the failure would merely move the local optimum; any successor needs a new causal hypothesis and held-out orders/phase shifts.
