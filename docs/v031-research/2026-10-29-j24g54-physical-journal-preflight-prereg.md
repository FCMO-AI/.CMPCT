# J24G54 — prospective physical journal/preflight divergence falsifier

**Registered 2026-10-29 UTC BEFORE instrument and result-bearing work.** This is an independent *research-only* child of J24G52 / PR #783, base commit `47ddedbeb844a8d59e9d142910c6705301214ce0`, not a duplicate Reader implementation. Primary claim to test: a genuinely Builder-created, native-xattr-bearing revision-24 archive with an actual committed `append_delete()` that removes an intervening sorted row can be logically sound to the ordinary Reader but rejected by `validation.preflight_archive()` because `validation._apply_delta` retains stale signed positional `fsmeta.xattrs` ordinals. Prior source-only #783 test is **O0 evidence** (not product proof). Product main, benchmarks, version, format, and release law are untouched.

## Primary question and exact disproof
Can *one exact immutable physical file* have (a) signed source attrs owned by a survivor after a journal deletion, (b) valid latest committed footer, (c) passing Reader verify and extraction with native restored xattrs, yet (d) fail structural preflight, or otherwise give a preflight vs Reader effective-index disagreement? Hypothesis **YES** for at least one delete/reorder shape, with an empty/no-reorder control **PASS** for both. A physical archive where both paths accept correct metadata **falsifies** the predicted false rejection. A Builder failing xattr capture, independent footer/integrity corruption, stale package vs source SHA, or missing native host support is an *instrument/infrastructure failure*, not evidence of the product claim.

## Controls, novelty and boundary
Compare *same source archive bytes* through independent Reader and preflight paths; ensure unchanged control preflight PASS, check the xattr exists in Builder index, then make real `append_delete` commit. Include an outside-implementation forensic decoder using external Zstandard/MessagePack to authenticate primary/header/body and the trailing journal, its SHA-256 and actual one-op deletion. Physical bytes must be stable between the two product reads. Independently mutate footer/delta commitments to prove the oracle rejects perturbations. Test both two-xattr and single survivor-xattr cases, on Linux and macOS; Windows is excluded from native xattr promises. Do not turn observed preflight false rejection into a Reader correctness false-accept claim. Do not weaken validators or rewrite historical #783 source-only test.

## PARETOBONK
The narrow existing #783 source-level discriminator already makes a second abstract model low-value. Rather than prematurely implement shared replay architecture, the cheapest decision-changing next rung is real Builder→Writer→Reader/preflight data plus a separately authenticated footer. Competing routes: copy Reader replay into validation (duplication, dangerous); import Reader into validation (circular import); extract common pure replay helper into independent module (credible future fix, but premature before physical proof); leave preflight partial (violates exactness semantics if physical proof succeeds). Primary selected rung: physical falsifier. No claims of generalized repair or full release parity without a distinct patch and full checks.
