## Primary question, hypothesis, stronger control
Can the functional union O80 preserve *complete real archive bytes* and acceptable full-Builder creation economics relative to exact O64 when both are fed **the same actual filesystem tree**? Prediction: valid source cases produce identical full CMPCT archive byte SHA-256 and length, with no systematic material creation regression from successful xattr reads or symlink inode tracking; the O80 guards add little work for admitted sources. ## Kill and narrow conditions / negatives
Any source-shape mismatch, different imported Builder SHA, nonzero archive-byte delta, different full-archive SHA, mismatch in content/link topology/xattrs, or any genuine reader/extractor failure defeats equality. If a platform cannot construct named xattrs or physical symlink external aliases, preserve explicit `UNSUPPORTED` rather than silently substituting an easier fixture. A candidate slowdown clearing both 5% and 3 ms in paired medians is **possible debt**, not an automatic release fail without replication and stronger runner controls. RSS and extraction shifts remain visible even if byte equality passes. Check global maintenance/integration cost: pending O52 mutable-source custody, O75 output atomicity and the larger official release matrix are out of scope and remain debt.
