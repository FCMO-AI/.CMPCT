## Active thesis — ONE-Genesis: Resource-Bounded Generative Information Graph

### Worldview delta

Historical CMPCT increasingly accumulated specialized exact representations and discovery mechanisms: direct codecs, deduplication, chunk maps, resemblance, EntropyGraph, Mosaic, residual programs, reversible container transforms and related research families. Even when adaptive selection prevents size regression, each persistent reader-visible mechanism can impose control bytes, dispatch, parser/fuzz surface, native parity burden, discovery cost and portability complexity.

CMPCT ONE proposes a different information model:

> **All useful exact information relationships should, where practical, compile into one bounded generative representation: Law + Surprise, with selective Crystallization.**

The reader receives the resolved explanation; it never reproduces the search that discovered it.

Conceptually:

```text
D = U(G, S)
```

where `D` is the exact logical information universe, `G` is the bounded Law graph, `S` is irreducible Surprise, and Crystals are selectively materialized derivable facts chosen when they improve the complete size/speed/access/recovery objective.

This is **not** F-01 revived with a larger grammar and it is **not** a wrapper around old codecs. Opaque reader opcodes such as `ZSTD()`, `MOSAIC()`, `PREFLATE()` or benchmark-family equivalents do not satisfy the thesis.

### Capability delta

If ONE survives, CMPCT gains a single reader-visible information model able to express and fuse relationships that are currently separated by representation boundaries. The desired consequences are:

1. historical mechanisms become encoder discovery knowledge rather than permanent reader ontologies;
2. deterministic derivation and statistical entropy coding become one Law/Surprise continuum;
3. multiple predictors may cooperate before Surprise rather than forcing file-level compressor selection;
4. latent compiler-invented Information Basis objects may explain several supplied objects more cheaply than any one supplied object can;
5. random access, parallelism and corruption locality become Crystallization/Reconstruction-Cone optimization constraints rather than separate compression subsystems;
6. future compression discoveries can improve Law discovery without necessarily changing stable reader semantics;
7. creation work can be allocated by expected information yield rather than brute-force portfolio execution.

### Core falsifiable thesis

> A small generic bounded ONE algebra plus a common Surprise semantics can represent the important exact relationships already exploited by CMPCT, then discover additional relationships, with complete representation/runtime cost low enough to produce a larger general-purpose size/speed/access Pareto frontier than the specialized frontier it replaces.

The first falsifier is deliberately much smaller than the full product claim:

> **Can one small generic ONE grammar express literal, reuse, repetition, sparse/fill and multi-source reconstruction exactly without control overhead or reader complexity becoming comparable to the separate mechanisms it is intended to subsume?**

If not, revise or retire the proposed IR before adding deep synthesis, format analyzers or native optimization.

### Why this clears the Thesis Initiation Gate

1. **Worldview delta:** specialized stored representations → one generative information graph.
2. **Capability delta:** cross-mechanism fusion, unified deterministic/statistical residual semantics, latent basis formation, compute/access-aware crystallization and stable-reader/future-compiler separation.
3. **Non-triviality:** this is not a threshold, codec switch or benchmark-specific admission patch.
4. **Prior evidence boundary:** EntropyGraph/Mosaic/reversible reconstruction establish useful exact relationships; F-01 establishes both real composition headroom and important generalization failures. ONE explicitly inherits those constraints rather than claiming them away.
5. **Plausible headroom:** current v0.29/v0.30 history demonstrates that multiple families remove real bytes but also accumulate selection/carrying cost; unifying their useful relationships can remove representation boundaries and may expose relationships that file/mechanism-local systems cannot exploit.
6. **Addressable opportunity hypothesis:** the basic Law vocabulary targets generic properties—identity, repetition, correlation, prediction, arithmetic/structural regularity and cross-object relationships—rather than a named benchmark class. Actual AOM remains to be measured.
7. **Cheap decisive oracle:** ONE-00/ONE-01 build a minimal IR/evaluator and translate simple existing relationships before any expensive productization.
8. **Disproof/retirement rule:** excessive control/reader complexity at ONE-01, failure to approach credible ordinary-data density/speed at subsequent gates, or inability to absorb existing structural wins without equivalent cost forces redesign or retirement rather than grammar inflation.
9. **Product survival sketch:** bounded deterministic dataflow execution, explicit resource preflight, Reconstruction Cones, Crystallization, native bulk kernels, one common Surprise channel and compiler-only rich analysis provide a plausible reader/locality/recovery path.
10. **Carrying-cost hypothesis:** ONE is only successful if its stable primitive/core burden is materially smaller and more compositional than the mechanism inventory it replaces; every new primitive must pay for its global reader/native/fuzz/portability cost.

## F-01 hostile review conclusion

The one-stage assumption remains falsified: exact composition is a real representational capability. What failed is the stronger claim that the current grammar plus the current human structural labels define a trustworthy general opportunity class.

The hostile off-grid win shows that the compiler can exploit useful heterogeneity outside the intended label boundary. The failed pruning result independently shows that seed-local operator inactivity cannot be promoted into a general simplification rule.

The review considered relabeling the hostile case, moving/densifying the split grid, adding operators, treating measured compression gain as the only admission predicate, and introducing a pre-search structural predictor. The first three are result-driven rescue moves and are rejected. Exact measured gain alone is an encoder tournament fact, not yet the missing causal account. A pre-result structural predictor could support a future superseding thesis, but repository evidence did not establish one under F-01.

Therefore no new F-01 freeze is authorized. See `docs/v030-rnd/F01_HOSTILE_THESIS_REVIEW.md`.
