## Falsifiable hypothesis H-OFFICE-PHYS-1

**H1:** Most of the remaining Office regret is introduced by the physical/locality productization layer (pack geometry, selective-read boundedness, authenticated/recovery framing), not by the implicit-v4 filesystem control plane itself.

### Disproof

H1 is disproved if, holding the physical payload membership and compressed payload bytes constant, replacing the explicit filesystem control plane with implicit-v4 recovers a material fraction of the Office deficit without violating the existing selective-read contract. For this attribution, "material" means >=10% of the measured same-run Office regret to the frozen v0.29 control; this threshold is preregistered only to classify the causal result, not to tune a product selector.

H1 is supported if the control-plane substitution is <10% of same-run regret and the dominant increase appears only when bounded locality/auth/recovery are charged.
