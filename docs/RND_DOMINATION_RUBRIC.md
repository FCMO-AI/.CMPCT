# 1. Forge objective

The Forge owns **known convergence problems**: measured product reds, strict competitor gaps, candidate productization, execution/search waste, rehabilitation and final release authority.

Where the active release contract requires it, true domination means:

> **Every frozen workload is strictly smaller and strictly faster to create than both ordinary ZIP/Deflate and solid Zstd-19, while preserving the accepted CMPCT product floor and every required semantic, safety, locality, recovery and platform invariant. Ties fail. Aggregate wins never erase a losing required row.**

This objective is product/convergence truth. It does not prohibit Foundry research unrelated to the current red rows.

# 2. Diagnose before editing: D0–D5

Every material Forge problem receives the strongest supported causal class before implementation.

## D0 — Evidence / custody red

The product result may be fine, but exact-head custody, workflow admission, artifact provenance, benchmark identity, runner setup or measurement is broken/missing.

**Response:** repair evidence/custody. Never invent a codec to fix CI.

## D1 — Local implementation overhead

Representation and algorithm are adequate; measured ordinary implementation cost can realistically close the gap.

Examples: copies, allocations, Python dispatch, syscalls, duplicate hashing, parser/FFI overhead.

**Response:** R1 optimization is admissible if the measured budget is sufficient.

## D2 — Execution architecture

The system repeatedly performs work whose fact could have one semantic owner: repeated scans, hashes, materializations, authentication, parsing, sorts or reconstruction.

**Response:** remove/fuse whole passes, change ownership, share exact operation-scoped facts, or move a real hot path into a more suitable implementation boundary.

## D3 — Search / admission architecture

Material work is spent constructing/evaluating candidates that lose or on unsafe heuristics that cannot terminate exactly.

**Response:** exact lower bounds, impossibility/necessary conditions, branch-and-bound, exact futility, single-pass admission or another algorithmic change that avoids paying for losers.

## D4 — Representation / physical-layout floor

Evidence shows the current representation family cannot satisfy the strict contract even under favorable assumptions, or repeated exported cost makes the boundary structurally non-Pareto.

**Response:** ordinary Forge R4 representation redesign is allowed if the problem remains inside the inherited information model. If the required fix changes what CMPCT considers an object/relation/explanatory source/reconstruction ontology, return the question to Foundry as an R5 thesis candidate rather than hiding R5 inside Forge work.

## D5 — Productization / platform

A mechanism already has strong candidate evidence, but generic admission, canonical semantics, recovery, locality, native/platform implementation, selector or exact release authority is incomplete.

**Response:** productize. Novelty does not outrank a real strict win.

A problem may span multiple classes. The highest-leverage causal class owns the next decisive hypothesis.

---

# 3. Forge radicality: R0–R4

- **R0 — measurement/disproof:** profiler, lower bound, exact contribution, impossibility, differential harness, causal attribution.
- **R1 — local optimization:** same representation/architecture; faster implementation.
- **R2 — execution architecture:** remove/fuse passes, change semantic ownership, native hot path, bounded parallelism.
- **R3 — algorithm/search:** exact terminals, branch-and-bound, admission/tournament redesign, proof-directed construction.
- **R4 — representation/physical semantics:** change physical grouping/representation within the current broad information model.

R5 belongs to the Foundry and is defined by `FUNDAMENTAL_RESEARCH_DOCTRINE.md`.

Use the **lowest tier capable of changing the causal fact that makes the product lose**. Higher radicality is not a virtue by itself.

---

# 4. Saturation / escalation triggers S1–S6

## S1 — Proven floor

If a valid optimistic floor shows the current representation family cannot cross the required competitor while preserving semantics, further threshold/metadata tuning cannot remain the primary path.

- minimum Forge response: R4;
- if the necessary representation changes the information model: `RETURN_TO_FOUNDRY`.

## S2 — Repeated low yield

If two consecutive material experiments in one family fail their preregistered materiality threshold or fail to close a meaningful fraction of the remaining gap, and no newly measured sufficient local owner exists, escalate one tier or retire the family.

Do not turn the exact historical 20% rule into a ritual when a better preregistered threshold exists; materiality must be tied to the actual gap/stage.

## S3 — Family retirement

After three falsified material hypotheses in one family, retire it as a primary Forge path unless new evidence changes the causal model, cost boundary, hardware regime or representation assumptions.

Retirement evidence must be **scoped** and carry reopening predicates per the finalized Foundry doctrine. A different threshold value is not new evidence.

## S4 — Exported-cost loop

If two rehabilitation attempts merely move debt among size/create/extract/locality/RSS or other required dimensions, change an architectural/representation boundary rather than continue scalar tuning.

## S5 — Speculative-work dominance

If a material fraction of a critical path is spent on candidates ultimately rejected, prioritize exact necessary conditions/futility/search redesign before merely accelerating speculative work.

The old >=25% signal remains a strong practical trigger, not a substitute for causal profiling.

## S6 — Proven-win productization

If a mechanism already has reproducible strict candidate evidence and remaining blockers are D5, Forge convergence has immediate priority. Foundry can continue independently, but it must not destabilize or strand the candidate.

# 5. Strict-gap ledger

Before material Forge work, recover exact current evidence for every dimension that can block promotion:

- CMPCT complete archive bytes;
- required competitor bytes;
- CMPCT complete creation time;
- required competitor creation time;
- accepted inherited CMPCT bytes/runtime floors;
- extraction/verification/RSS/selective-read constraints;
- locality/decode-unit/recovery/integrity/platform state;
- candidate mechanism and exact evidence fingerprint;
- D0–D5 diagnosis;
- minimum R0–R4 tier;
- recent hypotheses and their actual gap/stage closure;
- next cheapest decisive falsifier;
- productization debt vector.

Useful quantities include:

`size_gap_to_X = CMPCT_bytes - X_bytes`

`time_gap_to_X = CMPCT_create - X_create`

`gap_closure_fraction = (old_positive_gap - new_positive_gap) / old_positive_gap`

Do not optimize away a dimension already winning merely to improve another metric unless the complete product remains Pareto-valid under the release contract.

# 6. Forge Research Priority Score (RPS)

RPS is retained because Forge has a relatively concrete objective. It is **not** a Foundry genius score.

Maximum 100:

- **Domination/release necessity — 0–15**
- **Plausible upside — 0–20**
- **Root-cause fit — 0–15**
- **Generality/content-driven admission — 0–10**
- **Information gain/falsifiability — 0–15**
- **Decisive experiment efficiency — 0–10**
- **Composability/product survival — 0–10**
- **Simplicity/portability — 0–5**

Interpretation:

- 80–100: queue/execute unless a stronger D5 release blocker dominates;
- 65–79: strong candidate when prerequisites are ready;
- 50–64: run when cheap/control/unblocking;
- <50: no deep budget without new evidence.

Hard saturation rules override inflated scores.

# 7. Micro-optimization admissibility

R1 work is legitimate when it can actually win.

Before material R1 effort, answer:

1. Is the remaining gap small enough that the measured local owner can realistically close it?
2. Is the owner inside the honest benchmark boundary and removable without violating an invariant?
3. Has the family avoided S1–S4 saturation?

If no, R1 becomes supporting work only.

This preserves excellent near-boundary engineering while forbidding endless polishing of a losing floor.

---

# 8. Hypothesis alternatives

For a meaningful unresolved Forge red, consider at least three genuinely different solution classes **when useful**:

- simplest strong control;
- architecture/search alternative;
- representation/boundary alternative if diagnosis warrants it.

Do not implement three merely to satisfy process. Use cheap disproofs to kill weak branches.

The prior rule forcing a structural-red activation in every rolling three R&D activations is **historical and superseded**. Foundry continuity is now governed by the finalized Foundry heartbeat; Forge allocation follows causal urgency, D5 productization and exact current evidence.

---

# 9. Forge preregistration

Before a material performance/convergence experiment, freeze enough of the following to prevent result-driven reinterpretation:

- target family/workload and exact fingerprint where relevant;
- strict gaps and inherited floor;
- D0–D5 diagnosis;
- R0–R4 tier and S1–S6 trigger;
- RPS when it is being used for allocation;
- mechanism hypothesis;
- strongest simpler control;
- strongest failure explanation;
- invariants;
- predicted work/byte owner;
- materiality/disproof threshold;
- identity/equivalence checks;
- hidden costs: preprocessing, search, hashing, verification, metadata, recovery, RSS, temporary I/O, locality, decode unit and platform burden;
- success handoff/productization step;
- falsification retirement/pivot.

Once result-bearing execution begins, follow the finalized frozen-experiment immutability law. Material scientific changes require a new freeze rather than editing the old one.

# 10. Materiality

Forge progress should be large enough to change the product decision.

Examples of material outcomes:

- close a meaningful fraction of the remaining strict gap;
- cross a required boundary with margin;
- eliminate a dominant pass/work category;
- prove a reusable strict candidate win;
- prove a lower bound that retires a family;
- convert a large speculative search into exact proof-directed avoidance;
- materially close a release-blocking runtime/RSS/locality debt;
- produce decisive negative evidence that changes strategy.

Tiny absolute work is fully legitimate when it is sufficient to cross a genuinely near boundary. Do not use a fixed percentage as theater.

# 11. Referee → Builder → Hostile Reviewer

Every material Forge iteration retains the three-pass discipline.

## Referee / pre-mortem

Try to reject the idea before implementation:

- Is the diagnosed owner causal and big enough?
- Is there a floor that makes the tier insufficient?
- Are we accelerating work that should be eliminated?
- Can a simpler control capture the gain?
- Where will the cost reappear?
- Does locality/recovery/native/platform erase it?
- What result retires the family?

## Builder / decisive instrument

Build the smallest exact experiment capable of changing the decision. Preserve semantics and the full required cost boundary.

## Hostile Reviewer / post-mortem

Attempt to explain the result away:

- noise/order/cache/harness asymmetry?
- stale or wrong fingerprint?
- mandatory work moved outside timing?
- locality/RSS/recovery/reader complexity exported?
- fixture-specific admission?
- surrogate rather than product path?
- too small to matter?

Then record one explicit Forge decision:

`PROMOTE_NEXT_PREREQUISITE`, `REHABILITATE_DEBT`, `ITERATE_SAME_FAMILY`, `ESCALATE_RADICALITY`, `RETURN_TO_FOUNDRY`, `RETIRE_FAMILY`.

“Promising” is incomplete accounting.

# 12. Exact futility

Proof that work cannot win is a first-class Forge optimization.

Prefer:

`cheap exact facts -> optimistic lower bound / necessary condition -> prove candidate cannot win -> do not construct it`

instead of:

`construct -> compress -> measure -> discard`.

Heuristic cancellation is not equivalent. If stopping can change the winning artifact, it remains research until an exact or explicitly release-authorized invariant exists.

---

# 13. Global carrying cost during Forge admission

A mechanism that wins only when selected can still make the product worse globally.

Before canonical admission, charge or bound:

- prefilter/nomination work on non-winning data;
- losing auditions;
- metadata/descriptor tax;
- reader/parser/fuzz states;
- binary/startup cost;
- recovery/locality interactions;
- native/platform maintenance;
- interactions with the rest of the portfolio.

Prefer cheap necessary-condition filters and mechanisms that subsume old complexity. A strict local win does not automatically justify permanent portfolio entropy.

---

# 14. Productization chain

When a research mechanism survives Foundry handoff or an ordinary Forge R4 experiment, drive it through the actual chain required by current repository law, typically:

`generic admission -> canonical semantics -> reader/verification -> recovery/locality -> native/platform -> selector/product -> exact common-fingerprint external authority -> release authority`

Do not skip a prerequisite because the research result is exciting.

Do not make the mechanism wait for a more elegant future Foundry abstraction if it already has a bounded valid product path.

---

# 15. Breakthrough rehabilitation

A failed release gate means “not promotable yet,” not automatically “research failure.”

For a reproducible high-upside mechanism with ordinary performance/product debt:

1. preserve the exact seed and matrix;
2. identify the exported cost;
3. open explicit debt;
4. attempt portfolio/fallback selection;
5. isolate the exported stage;
6. change the representation boundary or counter-invent when necessary;
7. re-run the full product gates;
8. promote only when the inherited floor is restored while the gain remains.

Correctness, integrity/authentication, hostile-input/resource safety and truthful evidence are never debt.

---

# 16. Negative evidence

Forge retirements must preserve **scoped conditional constraints**, not slogans.

Record:

- mechanism family;
- tested regime;
- optimistic assumptions gifted;
- observed deficit/failure;
- causal interpretation;
- what remains unproven;
- reopening predicates.

This prevents both rediscovery of dead ends and dogmatic overgeneralization.

---

# 17. Final Forge law

The Forge exists to prevent the opposite failure of the Foundry: brilliant research that never becomes a better archive.

Diagnose the real red.

Use the lowest sufficient intervention.

Escalate when floors and repeated low yield prove optimization cannot win.

Eliminate speculative work rather than merely making losers faster.

Productize proven wins.

Charge global mechanism tax.

Preserve exact negative evidence.

Return true ontology problems to the Foundry rather than disguising them as local fixes.

And do not call the product finished until every current promotion requirement is honestly satisfied.
