This repository follows the canonical fallback copyright format for original FCMO AI work.

When a file, component, article, document, dataset, benchmark artifact, website element, or other original work does not carry a more specific valid copyright notice, use exactly:

## Scope

This is a fallback attribution rule, not a blanket claim over material FCMO AI does not own.

- A more specific work-level notice, contributor agreement, assignment, or third-party license/notice takes precedence for the material it covers.
- Third-party code, papers, datasets, media, models, fonts, trademarks, and other protected material retain their original rights and notices.
- Listing Javier Castellanos Peña as Founder of FCMO Group does not by itself make him an author or copyright holder of FCMO AI work.
- FCMO Group and FCMO AI may be used as project/brand identifiers around the notice, but must not replace the canonical fallback copyright holder line.
- This file does not itself grant a software or content license. Licensing remains governed by the repository's applicable license documents.

If FCMO later becomes a separate legal entity, historical authorship or ownership must not be silently rewritten; any change should reflect the actual legal arrangement and documented rights chain.
